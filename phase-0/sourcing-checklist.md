# Lead sourcing checklist — find 100 US shops with no website

> Goal: ~100 US local shops (≈50 barbershops/salons + ≈50 cafés/restaurants) that have **no real
> website** (social-only is exactly who we want), each with a usable **email address**. ~3–5 hours.

## Pick a metro first

Choose 1–2 US metros to start (denser = faster sourcing + a "local" feel in the email). Mid-size
metros are less email-saturated than NYC/LA. You can scale to more cities later.

## How to find them (free)

1. **Google Maps** — search the metro + category, one at a time:
   - Half A: `barber shop`, `hair salon`, `nail salon`, `beauty salon`
   - Half B: `coffee shop`, `cafe`, `restaurant`
2. For each result, check the **Website** field on the listing:
   - ✅ **No website**, or it only links to **Instagram/Facebook** → **add to list**.
   - ❌ Has a real site → skip (for now).
3. Get an **email**: check their Facebook "About" / Instagram bio / Google listing. If only a phone
   shows, note it (calls are a fallback, but email is the Phase 0 channel).
4. Grab: **name, city, email, Instagram, phone, 1 line on their vibe** (e.g. "fade specialists",
   "vegan brunch spot").

## Qualify (keep only good fits)

- ✅ Consumer-facing, walk-in business (people search it before visiting).
- ✅ Active (recent posts / reviews) — a real, living business.
- ✅ No real website.
- ❌ Skip: chains/franchises (corporate sites), pure B2B, regulated/sensitive businesses.

## CAN-SPAM note

You can email any US business (no opt-in needed), but every email needs your **physical postal
address** + a **working unsubscribe**. Keep a **suppression list** and never re-email anyone who opts out.

## Prioritize the first 20

Pick shops with **strong visuals** (good food/interior photos on their IG) — they make the most
impressive demo sites and the best screenshots.

## Then

- Drop each into `lead-tracker.csv` (one row per shop; set `niche` = A or B).
- For your first ~20, **send me (Claude) the name + niche + city + Instagram + vibe** and I'll generate
  each shop's demo site + a screenshot for you to attach to Email 1.
