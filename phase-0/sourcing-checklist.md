# Lead sourcing checklist — find 100 shops in your city

> Goal: a list of ~100 local shops (≈50 barbershops/salons + ≈50 cafés/restaurants) that have
> **no real website** (Instagram/Facebook-only is exactly who we want). ~2–4 hours of work.

## How to find them (free)

1. **Google Maps** — search your city + category, one at a time:
   - `מספרה`, `ברבר`, `מכון יופי`, `ציפורניים` (Half A)
   - `בית קפה`, `מסעדה`, `קפה` (Half B)
2. For each result, open the listing and check the **Website** field:
   - ✅ **No website**, or it just links to **Instagram/Facebook** → **add to list**.
   - ❌ Has a real site (wix/own domain) → skip (for now).
3. Grab: **name, address, phone, Instagram handle, 1 line on their vibe** (e.g. "fade specialists", "vegan brunch").
4. **Instagram** — search the location/area; lots of barbershops & cafés live only there.
   Note their handle + whether their bio has a real website link (no link = great lead).

## Qualify (keep only good fits)

- ✅ Consumer-facing, walk-in shop (people Google it / look it up before visiting).
- ✅ Active (recent posts / reviews) — they're a real, living business.
- ✅ No real website (or IG/FB only).
- ❌ Skip: chains/franchises (they have corporate sites), purely B2B, regulated/sensitive businesses.

## Prioritize for the first 20

Pick shops you can **walk into easily** (near you / near each other) and ones with **strong visuals**
(good food/interior photos on their IG) — those make the most impressive demo sites.

## Then

- Drop each into `lead-tracker.csv` (one row per shop, set `niche` = A or B).
- For your first ~20, **send me (Claude) the name + niche + Instagram + vibe** and I'll generate
  each shop's demo site + a screenshot for you to show.

## Tip

Do it in **clusters** — one street / neighborhood at a time — so your walk-in route is efficient
and you can mention "I just did the place down the road" (local proof compounds fast).
