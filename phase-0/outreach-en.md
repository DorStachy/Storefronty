# Cold-email sequence — English (Phase 0, USA / CAN-SPAM)

> 3 touches over ~8 days. **Auto-stop on any reply or unsubscribe.** Personalize the [brackets] per shop —
> personalization is the single biggest lever. Every email carries a real name, the business name, a
> **physical US postal address**, and a working **unsubscribe** (CAN-SPAM).

---

## Email 1 — Day 0

**Subject:** a website for [Shop Name] 👀

> Hi [First name],
>
> I noticed [Shop Name] doesn't have a website yet — so I went ahead and built you one. Here's a peek:
>
> [📸 screenshot]
>
> It's a real, working site, not a mockup. Tell me **one thing** you'd change — different photos, your
> hours, a section about [their specialty] — and I'll make the change and send you the live link so you
> can click around the real thing.
>
> No catch, nothing to pay to look. Just reply and tell me what you'd tweak 🙂
>
> Michael
> *Storefronty — websites for local businesses*
> [Business postal address] · [unsubscribe]

## When they reply asking for a change

> Done! I updated [the thing they asked]. Take a look: [live link]
>
> On that page you can request one more change, and if you like it, make it yours with your own domain.
> Any questions, just reply — I'm a real person on the other end.

## Objection handling (reply templates)

| They say | You reply |
|---|---|
| "How much is it?" | "The preview and the change are free. If you love it, plans start at $29/mo — everything included, unlimited changes. But take a look first, no pressure." |
| "I have Instagram / Facebook, that's enough" | "Social is great for regulars — but when someone Googles '[their category] near me,' there's nothing to find. A site catches the people already looking for you." |
| "I don't have time for this" | "That's the whole point — I do all of it. You just tell me what to change. You never touch it." |
| "Is this a scam?" | "Totally fair to ask. I'm Michael, Storefronty is a registered US company [address]. You pay nothing until you're sure, and there's a 14-day money-back guarantee." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Of course. I'll leave the live link up so you can play with it, and I'll check back in a few days. No rush." |
| "Take me off your list" | "Done — removing you now. All the best." *(suppress permanently, immediately)* |

---

## Rules

- **Suppress on:** any unsubscribe, "remove," angry reply, or legal note — permanently, before every send.
- Keep spam complaints **< 0.3%**; if a sending domain degrades, pause and warm a fresh one.
- One reply from a lead **stops the sequence** for that lead.
