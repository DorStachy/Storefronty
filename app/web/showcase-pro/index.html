Our story

It started with one stubborn sourdough starter.

Marigold began as a Sunday market stall — one oven, one battered espresso machine, and a line that wrapped the corner. A decade on, we still bake everything in-house and still know most names that walk through the door.

We cook simply and seasonally: produce from growers we shake hands with, coffee from a roaster two streets over, and a pastry case that changes with the weather. Come for the cardamom buns; stay for the slow afternoon.
