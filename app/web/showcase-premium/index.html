The house

Restraint, then
quiet abundance.

We cook for the table, not the camera — flavors that arrive in their season and leave a little too soon.

Chef Aurélie Vance opened Maison Noir to chase a single idea: that an evening can be plated. The menu changes when the market does, the wine pairings are argued over nightly, and the dining room is kept deliberately small — forty seats, one service.

Everything that can be made in-house is: the cultured butter, the aged garums, the bread pulled warm to your table. The rest we source from a short list of growers, divers, and foragers we trust like family.
